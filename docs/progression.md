# Progression
{: .no_toc }

Track what players do and reward them for it: numbers that Steam keeps for each player, and achievements that pop up in the corner of the screen.

| I want to... | Go here |
|---|---|
| **Count things** per player (games played, enemies defeated, distance) | [Stats](stats.html) |
| **Unlock achievements** and show progress toward them | [Achievements](achievements.html) |
| **Rank players** by score or time, globally or among friends | [Leaderboards](leaderboards.html) |

All three read their rows from the [App Definition](core-concepts.html#the-app-definition). The [Quick Start](quick-start.html) unlocks your first achievement in 10 minutes.
